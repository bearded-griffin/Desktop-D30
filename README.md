# Desktop-D30
Professional Label Design for Phomemo D30

Tired of building labels on your phone? Desktop-D30 is a native Linux and Windows application designed to give you total creative control over your Phomemo D30 printer. 

  🚀 Key Features:
   * Intuitive Canvas: Drag-and-drop text, shapes, QR codes, and barcodes with pixel-perfect precision. 
   * Smart Barcoding: Built-in support for high-resolution QR codes and Code 128-B barcodes—ensuring every scan is
     successful.
   * Batch Printing (CSV Power): Perfect for small businesses! Import a .csv file to print hundreds of unique labels
     (address labels, price tags, or inventory stickers) in seconds.
   * Auto-Incrementing Fields: Set up labels with sequential serial numbers or dates that update automatically with every
     print.
   * Asset Manager: Build your own library of icons and decorative borders. Organize them into categories and search through
     them as you design.
   * Custom Fonts: Use any .ttf or .otf font on your system—or import your own for a truly unique look.

  🎨 Design Your Way:
   * Deco Borders: Choose from a library of built-in decorative frames or import your own.
   * Dark Mode & Grid: A professional interface that stays out of your way, complete with alignment tools and a beautiful
     dark theme.
   * Project Saving: Save your designs as .d30 files to edit or reuse them later.
